## 💡 What Interviewers Evaluate in HLD

During an HLD interview, it is rarely about getting the "perfect" answer. The interviewer is evaluating you on:

1. **Problem Breakdown:** How you navigate open-ended, ambiguous problems.
2. **Thought Process:** Thinking out loud and explaining *why* you are doing something.
3. **Communication:** Clarity in drawing components and explaining data flows.
4. **Trade-off Analysis:** There are no perfect designs, only trade-offs. You must justify your choices (e.g., "I chose Cassandra over PostgreSQL because we need heavy write throughput, and we can tolerate eventual consistency").
5. **Component Knowledge:** Deep understanding of when to use Kafka vs RabbitMQ, Redis vs Memcached, etc.
6. **Scalability:** Knowing exactly where the system will bottleneck as traffic 10x's, and how to fix it.
7. **Failure Handling:** What happens when a node dies? How does the system recover?
8. **Data Modeling:** Structuring your database schema and choosing partition keys wisely.
